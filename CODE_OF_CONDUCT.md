# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Anvil to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive project.
Participants must:

* communicate with empathy and respect;
* assume good intent while addressing impact;
* give and accept actionable feedback;
* protect private information; and
* focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats,
personal attacks, sexualised language or attention, publishing private
information, and any other conduct that would reasonably make participation
unsafe.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the
contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include sensitive details
in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled
confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, restrict
participation, or take other action proportionate to the circumstances.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and wherever an individual is
representing the project.
